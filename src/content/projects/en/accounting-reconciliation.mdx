## Technical decisions

Reusable normalization components, a separation between components, pages, and business rules, and the Page Object Model kept browser automation navigable. Structured logging made diagnosis part of the workflow.

## Reliability

Browserless, structured logging, and modularity are confirmed resilience and maintenance choices. There is no publishable historical measurement for retries, backoff, timeout, idempotency, deduplication, completeness, alerting, or partial failures.

## Results

The pipeline has three verified stages: collection, normalization, and consolidation. Time savings were confirmed qualitatively for the activity, but no historical measurement supports a number.

## Learnings

Reliable reconciliation begins with clear contracts between collection and transformation. The public description follows the same discipline: it shows the flow without exposing financial sources, data, or internal destinations.
