## Technical decisions

Authentication refresh is separated from the full run. Python coordinates extraction, consolidation, and delivery, while Playwright connects remotely to Browserless. Session state is reused without disclosing its contents.

## Reliability

Scheduled renewal and state reuse reduce dependence on a fresh sign-in for every run. This case deliberately does not claim availability, success-rate, retry, backoff, timeout, idempotency, or alerting metrics because they have no historical or publishable measurement.

## Results

The publishable numeric evidence is a 1-hour cookie-refresh cadence. It describes an operational routine; it is not evidence of time saved, availability, or business impact.

## Learnings

Authentication belongs in the operating design, not as an implementation afterthought. Evidence limits matter too: this account contains no portal details, credentials, identifiers, or collected data.
