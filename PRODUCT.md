# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the smallest first version and straightforward GitHub Pages deployment. Revisit if a framework becomes necessary.

## Users

Visitors evaluating Raffael Henrique's work, background and ways to connect.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio makes Raffael Henrique's profile, data engineering work and professional material easy to understand and explore. Success means a visitor can identify him, understand his focus and reach his projects or contact channel quickly.

## Positioning

Raffael Henrique is a data engineer with 6+ years of experience who connects data, automation, engineering and applied AI to solve real business problems.

## Operating Context

Public web experience on desktop and mobile. The portfolio will be built incrementally, with one visible surface added and reviewed at a time.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The first surface contains a reusable shell, profile sidebar, primary navigation, overview content and theme switching.
- The visual structure is modeled on https://alexeygrigorev.com/.
- No backend, build pipeline or third-party dependency is required for the first version.
- Courses are not part of the portfolio and should be removed from the product.
- Projects will come from GitHub. The Kestra project is private for now and should not be exposed until access is provided.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the referenced portfolio as the structural and visual reference.
- Keep the interface content-first, restrained and easy to scan.
- Use Portuguese copy and Raffael's real professional context.

## Evidence on Hand

- Reference site: https://alexeygrigorev.com/
- Profile photo supplied at `assets/profile.jpg`.
- Professional copy and contact links supplied by the owner in chat.

## Product Principles

- Clarity before decoration.
- Build and review one surface at a time.
- Prefer native web capabilities and zero unnecessary dependencies.
- Use real content when supplied; keep open content visibly provisional.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Use semantic landmarks and headings.
- Keep all controls keyboard accessible with visible focus states.
- Maintain readable contrast in both themes.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
